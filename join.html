Who We Are Looking For

Motivated students with a background in one or more of: semiconductor device physics, microelectronic fabrication, RF/microwave measurement, or experience with TCAD, IC-CAP or ADS.

Students work hands-on with temperature-dependent DC, pulsed, noise and 67 GHz RF measurements, and with compact modeling of Wide-Bandgap Semiconductor Devices and HEMTs.

How to Apply

  1. Apply to the ECE graduate program. Submit through the TTU Graduate School and list Dr. Kim as a faculty member of interest.
  2. Email Dr. Kim. Include your CV, transcripts and a short paragraph on which research area fits you.
  3. Undergraduates at TTU can ask about research assistant positions during the semester.
